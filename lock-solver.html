🔐 Lockpicking Solver

Enter your lock's plate positions and linkages, then hit Solve to get the exact slide sequence. Read the lockpicking guide for how to discover a lock's linkages.

1 · Lock setup

Click a hole to place the pin where it currently sits in-game. Hole 4 is the center (goal).

2 · Plates & linkages

For each plate, click the tags to mark which other plates move when you slide this plate — Same = moves the same way, Opp = moves the opposite way, — = not linked.

3 · Solve

How the game works: each plate has 7 holes and one gold pin. Slide every pin into the center (hole 4). Plates can be linked, so moving one can shift others. A move that pushes a pin past hole 1 or 7 breaks the lockpick — this solver only ever gives legal moves.
