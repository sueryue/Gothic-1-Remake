🎮 New to Gothic 1 Remake?

Your first 30 minutes, step by step

1️⃣

Follow Diego

He escorts you safely to the Old Camp. Complete his tutorial quest for your first real weapon.

2️⃣

Loot the Path

Old Sword under the hanged man, Hunter's Knife for Drax (free Shortbow). No weight limit — grab everything.

3️⃣

Train Early

Scatty unlocks the parry (10 LP + 50 ore). Fingers teaches lockpicking — the two best LP investments in the game.

4️⃣

Don't Commit Yet

Clear all three camps' quests before choosing. Save before the faction oath — it's permanent.

❓ Need Help?

Common questions answered

Which faction is best?

Old Camp for your first run. New Camp for archers. Swamp Camp for unique playthroughs.

How does lockpicking work?

Sliding plates. 4-7 plates, gold pins, interconnected moves. Train with Fingers for more mistakes.

Can I switch camps?

No. It's permanent. Complete all three camps' quests before committing, then save.
