🎮 New to Gothic 1 Remake?

Your first 30 minutes, step by step

1️⃣

Follow Diego

He escorts you safely to the Old Camp. Complete his tutorial quest for your first real weapon.

2️⃣

Loot the Path

Old Sword under the hanged man, Hunter's Knife for Drax (free Shortbow). No weight limit — grab everything.

3️⃣

Train Early

Scatty unlocks the parry (10 LP + 50 ore). Fingers teaches lockpicking — the two best LP investments in the game.

4️⃣

Don't Commit Yet

Clear all three camps' quests before choosing. Save before the faction oath — it's permanent.

❓ Need Help?

Common questions answered

Which faction is best?

Old Camp for your first run. New Camp for archers. Swamp Camp for unique playthroughs.

How does lockpicking work?

Sliding plates. 4-7 plates, gold pins, interconnected moves. Train with Fingers for more mistakes.

Can I switch camps?

No. It's permanent. Complete all three camps' quests before committing, then save.

💡 Tip of the Day

Fresh advice, updated regularly

Save Before Every Lock

Every lock's linkage pattern is fixed — test each plate once, note what moves, reload, then solve correctly. Untrained characters get only 2 mistakes. Push a pin to the edge (position 1 or 7) and the lockpick breaks. Train with Fingers in the Old Camp. Full lockpicking guide →

⚠️ Don't Miss These

Permanently missable content — the mistakes that cost hours

Don't Kill Mordrag

The single most costly decision in the game. Convince him to leave — he escorts you to New Camp and you get 2,000+ free XP. Killing him loses all New Camp intro quests.

Save Before the Faction Oath

Faction choice is permanent and locks you out of the other camps' quests, armor, and companions. Complete all three camps' pre-join quests first, then save.

Take Lester to the Fortress

Clear the Mountain Fortress with Lester, or the "Special Pieces" quest breaks permanently and you lose the Title Deed.

Buy Before Chapter Transitions

Permanent boosters (Dragonroot, Goblin Berries) vanish from merchant inventories every chapter transition. Buy them before advancing the main story.

🏰 About Gothic 1 Remake Wiki

Gothic 1 Remake launched June 5, 2026, and this wiki was built by a player who finished it multiple times — not from press previews. Every guide, lock solution, and strategy here was tested in the actual game.

The Remake is a ground-up rebuild of Piranha Bytes' 2001 classic by Alkimia Interactive, published by THQ Nordic. It keeps the no-hand-holding design — no minimap, no quest markers — but modernizes combat, lockpicking, and visuals. This wiki helps you find your way without spoiling the discovery.
