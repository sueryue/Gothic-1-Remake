About Gothic 1 Remake

Gothic 1 Remake is a modern rebuild of the 2001 cult classic RPG by Piranha Bytes. Same unforgiving world, same faction politics — rebuilt from the ground up by Alkimia Interactive and published by THQ Nordic.

Stuck on the Collapsed Tower lock? Can't decide between Old Camp and Swamp Camp? The tools and guides here will get you sorted.

What's Actually on This Site

I started this wiki after spending way too many hours stuck on the same lock puzzle in the Collapsed Tower. Turns out the Remake's lock system is nothing like the original — you're not just tapping left and right anymore, you're memorizing color sequences and hoping you don't brick the chest. After the third broken lockpick I figured there had to be a better way, so I wrote a solver. Then I wrote more guides. Then it became this whole site.

Picking a Faction (Don't Rush This)

Every new player I've talked to has the same regret: they joined a camp too early and missed half the side quests. Each faction lets you run errands for them before you commit — Old Camp has something like seven pre-join quests, New Camp has five or six, and the Swamp Camp guys have their own chain too. Do all of them first. You'll get more XP, better starting gear, and you'll actually know which vibe you prefer before locking yourself in forever.

Old Camp is the straightforward choice — heavy armor, melee combat, Fire Mage magic in the late game. New Camp plays more like a rogue/thief hybrid and their water mages are weirdly practical. Swamp Camp is the wildcard; their armor looks ridiculous but their alchemy recipes are broken good. I went Old Camp my first run and ended up restarting after ten hours because I wanted to try the Swamp Camp's weed-powered build.

Still can't decide? The Faction Quiz asks three questions and gives you an honest recommendation. No "it depends" cop-outs.

The Lock Solver (Because the Puzzle Locks Are Brutal)

Here's how the lock system actually works in the Remake. Each lock has a bunch of colored buttons. You need to press them in the right order. Each wrong press costs you a lockpick and damages the lock — too many mistakes and the chest is permanently jammed. Some of these chests have unique weapons or quest items inside, so breaking one sucks.

The Lockpicking Solver just works — type in the colors you see, it spits out the sequence. No guessing, no broken lockpicks. I built it with BFS so it finds the shortest possible solution every time. People tell me the Collapsed Tower puzzle went from "I quit" to "done in thirty seconds" after they found the solver.

Besides the solver, there's a whole Lockpicking Guide with diagrams if you want to actually learn the mechanic instead of just getting the answer. And the Calculator works for the simpler locks where you just need combination math.
