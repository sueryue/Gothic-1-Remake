What This Site Is

Gothic 1 Remake launched June 5, 2026, and I've played through it multiple times. The first time I played, I got stuck on the Collapsed Tower lock for almost an hour — broke two lockpicks before I realized the system is completely different from the original. The original had you listening for tumblers. The Remake gives you sliding plates where moving one plate shifts others. After figuring it out, I built this site to share everything I learned — faction choices, build plans, and how to solve every lock.

Picking Your Faction

All three camps let you complete their quests before you commit. Old Camp (ruled by Gomez, central location, most merchants) is the friendliest for a first playthrough — Diego guides you, the quest path is clear, and Fire Mages can reach Circle 6 magic. New Camp (led by mercenary boss Lee, west side) is best for dexterity/archer builds — higher quest rewards, Water Mages with crowd-control ice magic, and you avoid the Chapter 4 banishment. Swamp Camp (Brotherhood of the Sleeper, east marshes) offers the Templar spellsword hybrid — psionic magic ignores armor, but is hard-capped at Circle 4.

Can't decide? The Faction Quiz asks three questions and gives you a straight recommendation.

Lockpicking — The System That Got Me

The Remake uses a sliding plate puzzle: each lock has 4-7 plates, each plate has 7 holes and a gold pin. The goal is to center every pin at the 4th position. The catch: moving one plate often moves others — some in the same direction, some opposite, some not at all. Push a pin past position 1 or 7 and the lockpick breaks. Untrained characters get 2 mistakes. Train with Fingers (Old Camp, behind the arena) for 4 mistakes (10 LP + 100 ore) or Master level for 6 (20 LP + 200 ore).

Strategy: save first, test each plate to see the linkages, solve the most-connected plates first, save independent plates for last. Lockpicking Guide has the full tutorial. The Lock Solver is an interactive demo tool.
