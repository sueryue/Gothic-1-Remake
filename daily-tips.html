Tip of the Day — August 1

💡 Save Before Every Lock

Every lock's linkage pattern is fixed — test each plate once to see what moves, note it down, then reload and solve with the correct sequence. Untrained characters only get 2 mistakes. Push a pin to the edge (position 1 or 7) and the lockpick breaks. Train with Fingers in the Old Camp. Try the Lock Solver for help.

What to Do Right After Starting

Your first character will probably die to a Scavenger within ten minutes — that's normal. The Colony does not care about you. Here's what the game doesn't tell you.

From the Beach to Old Camp — Free Loot

Diego gives you a tutorial quest at the exchange point — do it. Along the path, grab: Old Sword (under the hanged man), Club (near Juvenile Scavenger corpse), Hunter's Knife (same path, give to Drax for a free Shortbow). There's an underwater cave in the river near Drax's camp — swim in for a Rider Bow, one of the best early ranged weapons.

Near Old Camp, find a guard complaining about a ring he dropped in the swamp — two minutes south, return it for your first real ore. Pick every herb you see. King's Sorrel, Dragonroot, Swampweed — they stack infinitely, weigh nothing, and cooks/alchemists pay better than regular merchants.

Combat: Don't Face-Tank Anything

At level 1, a single wolf can kill you in three hits. Core loop: lock on, backpedal until they whiff, step in for 1-2 hits, back out. Don't combo. Each enemy type has a different attack rhythm — learn them one at a time. If it has more legs than you or glows in the dark, run.

Wolves hunt in packs — see one, assume three more nearby. Creep forward, pull one, retreat. Shadowbeasts will delete you at low levels. Just sprint and find a ledge to climb.

Picking Your Faction

All three camps let you complete quests before committing — so you can test each one first. For the full comparison (including exact join requirements, magic caps, armor rewards, and the interactive quiz), see the Complete Faction Guide.

Quick summary: Old Camp for first playthroughs (Diego leads, fire magic, clear path). New Camp for archer/economy builds (best rewards, water magic). Swamp Camp for the most unique experience (Templar spellsword, psionic magic).

Make a manual save before swearing allegiance. This is the single most important save in the game — it lets you replay other factions without redoing the early game. Use the Faction Quiz if you're stuck.

Lockpicking

The Remake uses a sliding plate puzzle (4-7 plates per lock, gold pins, interconnected plates). For the full system breakdown — training costs, solving strategy, mistake tolerance, lockpick sources — see the Complete Lockpicking Guide.

Speedrun / Multi-Playthrough Tips

⚡ Fast Chapter 1 Route (8 hours)

Diego guides → Old Camp safehouse → Scatty (parry, 10LP+50ore) → Fingers (lockpicking, 10LP+100ore) → Mordrag to New Camp (2,000XP free) → Swampweed smuggling → Baal Parvez to Swamp Camp → Complete all three camp quests → Manual save at decision point.

📊 Optimal LP Formula

Levels 1-30: each stat costs 1 LP. After level 30: costs skyrocket. So dump ALL LP into your main stat before 30. Trained lockpicking (10LP) is always the #1 priority. Then weapon skill. Then main stat to 30. Secondary stats after 30.

⏰ Achievement Route

Playthrough 1 (Old Camp/Fire Mage): Insomnia Champion + Mercy Over Madness + Mud Freak. Playthrough 2 (New Camp/Water Mage): True Stranger + Anatomaniac + Map Sniffer. Playthrough 3 (Swamp Camp/Templar): all faction-exclusive achievements. Key save: Chapter 1 faction decision point (perm save).
