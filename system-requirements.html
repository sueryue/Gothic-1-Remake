💡 Optimization Tips

  • Turn on DLSS or FSR — biggest FPS boost on both Nvidia and AMD GPUs. Quality mode looks nearly identical to native.
  • Shadow quality is the biggest FPS drain — dropping shadows from Ultra to High can gain 10-15 FPS.
  • Disable motion blur — improves clarity and can help framerate on lower-end GPUs.
  • Use the SSD — the game streams textures heavily; an HDD causes stuttering, especially in the Old Mine.
  • Update GPU drivers — the game uses Unreal Engine 5's Lumen, which benefits significantly from recent driver optimizations.

🎮 Platform Notes

🖥️ PC

Steam required. Supports mouse & keyboard and controller. Ultrawide support confirmed. Mod support via Nexus.

🎮 PS5

Performance and Quality modes. Performance targets 60 FPS at 1440p; Quality targets 30 FPS at 4K with ray tracing.

🎮 Xbox Series X|S

Series X similar to PS5; Series S targets 1080p. Smart Delivery supported.

Note: System requirements were provided by the developer at launch. Actual performance varies with drivers, background apps, and game patches. See PCGamingWiki for community-tested fixes and settings.
