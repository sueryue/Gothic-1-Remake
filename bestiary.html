📖 Bestiary

Every creature in the Valley of Mines — know your enemy

⚠️ Golden Rule: In Gothic 1 Remake, not everything is meant to be fought when you first see it. Knowing when to run is the most important survival skill.

Combat Survival Guide

Levels 1-3

Avoid all combat. Even a wolf can kill you. Pick herbs, avoid fights entirely.

Levels 4-6

Scavengers, juvenile wolves, and lone molerats are manageable. Still avoid wolf packs and large creatures.

Levels 7-10

Most wildlife is fair game. Shadowbeasts and orcs remain dangerous. Use terrain to your advantage.

Levels 10+

You can handle most enemies. Large groups and bosses still require preparation.

Creatures

🐺 Wolf

Easy

Weakness: Fire, Piercing. Packs of 3-5 are deadly below level 5. Lure one at a time, dodge sideways. Drops: wolf skin, raw meat. Level: 3+

🦅 Scavenger

Easy

Weakness: Piercing, Blunt. Tutorial enemy. Wait for peck animation, dodge back, counter-attack. Drops: feathers, raw meat. Level: 1+

🐗 Molerat

Easy

Weakness: Blunt, Fire. Slow and predictable — perfect for practicing combos. Charge attack is well telegraphed. Drops: molerat teeth, raw meat. Level: 1+

🦟 Bloodfly

Easy

Weakness: Fire, Piercing. Hard to hit with melee — use bow or wait for dive. Drops: bloodfly wings. Level: 5+

👹 Goblin

Easy

Weakness: Any element, Piercing. Come in groups of 3-5. Individually weak but dangerous in numbers. Drops: goblin trinkets, small ore. Level: 4+

🦎 Field Raider (Lurker)

Medium

Weakness: Piercing, Fire. Ambush predator in tall grass. Quick and aggressive. Watch for movement in vegetation. Drops: lurker claws, leather. Level: 6+

🦖 Snapper

Medium

Weakness: Blunt, Piercing. Fast and aggressive. Dodge to the side, not backward. Bite combo hits 3 times. Drops: snapper teeth, claws, leather. Level: 7+

🕷️ Minecrawler

Medium

Weakness: Blunt, Fire. Heavy armor, resistant to slashing. Use maces/hammers or fire magic. Found in Old Mine. Drops: minecrawler mandibles (valuable), ore chunks. Level: 8+

💀 Skeleton

Medium

Weakness: Blunt, Blessed. Resistant to piercing. Use maces or blessed weapons. Come in groups guarding ancient tombs. Drops: bone fragments, ancient trinkets. Level: 10+

🐺 Warg

Medium

Weakness: Fire. Stronger wolf variant in later areas. Same pack tactics but higher stats. Drops: warg skin, large meat. Level: 10+

👻 Undead

Hard

Weakness: Silver, Fire, Blessed. First encountered in Chapter 3 depths. Immune to fear/mental magic. Fire magic is the best counter. Drops: ancient coins, unique rings. Level: 12+

🐺 Shadowbeast

Hard

Weakness: Fire, Silver. Extremely dangerous. Do NOT fight below level 10. Use fire magic scrolls if melee. Massive damage lunge attack. Drops: shadowbeast horn (quest item), beast skin. Level: 12+

👹 Orc Warrior

Hard

Weakness: Fire, Blessed/Silver. Heavy armor, high HP, massive damage. Use parry and punish their slow recovery. Drops: orc weapon, orc armor shards. Level: 15+

🪨 Troll

Hard

Weakness: Fire. Massive HP pool, slow but devastating attacks. Use ranged to weaken, then melee finish. Ground slam has large AoE. Drops: troll hide (best leather), troll heart. Level: 18+

👹 Orc Elite

Hard

Weakness: Fire, Silver. Endgame enemy in Orc Temple. Even stronger than regular orcs. Bring 20+ healing items. Use hit-and-run. Drops: elite orc weapon, valuable ore. Level: 20+

For original Gothic veterans: Several enemies have redesigned attack patterns in the remake. Shadowbeasts and trolls are significantly harder than in the original. The undead and skeleton enemy types are new additions unique to the remake. Plan your builds accordingly.
