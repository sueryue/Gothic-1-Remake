🎮 Trophy Structure

The trophy/achievement list covers more than the main story — it pushes you into camp membership, guild ranks, weapon mastery, magic lessons, crafting, lockpicking, and challenge runs.

🟣 PlayStation

41 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 20 Silver, 18 Bronze.

💻 PC / Xbox

42 achievements (same list as PS minus the Platinum, plus platform variants).

🌐 No Online

No online or co-op requirements. No difficulty-based trophies.

Tip: Expect 60-80 hours for 100% completion. You'll need at least three playthroughs (one per faction) because each camp has exclusive achievement lines. Save before the faction oath in Chapter 1 to make replays faster. See the 100% Completion Route.
