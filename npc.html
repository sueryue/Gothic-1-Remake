👥 NPC Database

Key characters — locations, roles, services

✅ Playtested ✓ NPCs verified Locations and roles confirmed across playthroughs

🔥 Old Camp

Diego

Mentor / Main Quest Giver. Trains STR & DEX. Found on beach path, then Old Camp.

Gomez

Old Camp Leader. Controls ore trade. Final admission decision.

Thorus

Guard Captain. Combat missions, patrol routes, admission contact.

Torrez

Fire Mage trainer. Sells runes, mana upgrades. Quest "The Price of Magic".

Scatty

One-Handed trainer. Unlocks parry (10 LP + 50 Ore).

Fingers

Lockpicking trainer. Trained 10 LP+100 Ore (4 mistakes). Master 20 LP+200 Ore (6).

Snaf

Cook. Recipe quest gives 3 free Meatbug Ragouts daily.

Graham

Map seller. Old Camp detailed map + Colony map.

💧 New Camp

Lee

Mercenary Leader. Bandit/mercenary quests, admission contact.

Wolf

Bow trainer. Teaches archery, sells bows.

Saturas

Water Mage leader. Free Mine secret project.

Swiney

Free Mine. Gives free Digger's Dress armor.

🌀 Swamp Camp

Namib, Tyon, Orun, Cadar

The Gurus. Need approval from 4 of 5 to join.

Fortuno

3 free Swampweed rolls daily.

Gor Na Drak

Templar weapon trainer. Two-handed weapons.

Baal Parvez

Old Camp Guru. Escorts you safely to Swamp Camp.

⚠️ NPC Mortality: NPCs have real routines and can be killed by monsters. If a trainer dies, their services are permanently lost. Reload a save.
