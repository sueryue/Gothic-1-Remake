    📸 Visual Walkthrough

    Learn through gameplay — less reading, more seeing

    ⚠️ Screenshots are still being gathered from actual gameplay. For now, use the text overview below or head to the Full Walkthrough and Daily Tips for detailed steps.

    🎬 Opening: Beach to Old Camp

    Follow Diego, loot everything (no weight limit). Key items: Old Sword (under hanged man), Club (near Scavenger corpse), Hunter's Knife (return to Drax for free Shortbow). Underwater cave near Drax has Rider Bow.

    🏕️ Choose Your Faction

    Old Camp (🔥) — Central, Diego leads you here. Fire Mages, Circles 1-6.

    New Camp (💧) — West. Mordrag escorts you. Mercenaries, Water Mages, best economy.

    Swamp Camp (🌀) — East marshes. Baal Parvez escorts you. Templar spellsword hybrid.

    🔐 Lockpicking

    Sliding plate system — not buttons. See the Lockpicking Guide for full details.
