🗺️ Map & Exploration Guide

Routes, landmarks, and survival navigation through the Valley of Mines

Valley Overview

Gothic 1 Remake is a seamless open world — three camps spread across the valley, trapped by a magical barrier. No minimap. No quest markers. You navigate by memory, landmarks, and hand-drawn maps bought from NPCs.

Buying Maps

  • Graham (Old Camp, north gate) — sells Old Camp detailed map + broader Colony map
  • Dexter (Old Camp market) — Colony map, cheaper than Graham's
  • Maps are hand-drawn sketches — they show settlements and roads, not GPS.

Safe Routes Between Camps

Old Camp → New Camp

Main road west. Have Mordrag escort you — he kills enemies, you get all XP (2,000+ per trip).

Old Camp → Swamp Camp

East through the marshes. Let Baal Parvez escort you — he safely navigates dangerous areas.

Exploration Golden Rules

  • Stick to roads early — beasts avoid paths
  • See a wolf, assume three more — pull one, retreat, fight alone
  • Ignore Shadowbeasts and Snappers at low levels — run, don't look back
  • Save before entering new areas
  • Enable Object Outliner in Accessibility settings
